# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in AllyRadar welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Focus feedback on the code, documentation, and behavior.
- Protect private information, credentials, server data, and player location data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, disclosure of private data, and deliberately unsafe or malicious contributions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Security and privacy vulnerabilities must follow `SECURITY.md` rather than a public issue.
